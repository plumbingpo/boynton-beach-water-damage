Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Runoff should flow away from your foundation, so fix low spots and add downspout extensions
  • Cracked caulk lets blown rain in, so inspect and renew the seals on doors and windows
  • Install impact-resistant windows or storm shutters if you live in a hurricane-prone area
  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
  • Take dated photos of the house every year, since insurers ask about prior condition

Signs You Need Storm Damage Repair in Boynton Beach

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Wind-driven rain coming in through windows, doors or wall penetrations
  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard
